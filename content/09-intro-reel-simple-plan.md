# Intro Reel: "46 followers, posting anyway" (simple, solo, ~15 sec)

Format follows the "first post, let's see where this goes" reels (R28 @asha.lifelately, R29 @createdbyjunior, R33). Note: these notes are based on their captions only. I have not seen their videos. To match one exactly, send a screen recording or describe it.

1. "46 followers. Posting anyway."
2. "I blocked everyone I know. Hi, strangers."
3. "You're one of my first 46."
4. "Nobody knows I made this page."
5. "Posted before I could talk myself out of it."
6. "Day zero of nothing in particular."  *(skip if you don't want any counter feel)*

Recommendation: #1 for the clearest hook, #3 for the warmest.

## VOICEOVER (about 12-15 sec, record as a voice note)

> I have 46 followers.

> So here I am, putting myself out there.
> Let's see where this goes.

(Update the follower number to the exact count right before you post.)

## SHOTS (5 clips, all alone, phone only)
Set the phone upright against a stack of books, facing a window. Press record, do the action, stop. 5-8 seconds each; you'll use 2-3.
1. **Your profile on screen:** screen-record your own page showing "46 followers" (for the hook).
2. **Chai/tea steaming** near the window.
3. **You from behind**, walking to the window or sitting at the desk.
4. **Hands typing or holding the phone.**
5. **Face to camera**, small awkward smile, say "let's see where this goes." Hold still for 2 seconds after.

No clips with addresses, number plates or other people's information.

## EDIT (CapCut, plain and simple)
1. Import the voiceover first. Put it on the timeline.
2. Lay the clips over it, in this order: profile screen, tea, walking, typing, face.
3. Trim each clip to about 2-3 seconds so the cuts follow your lines.
4. Add the text hook as a big text card for the first 2 seconds.
5. Add auto-captions, then fix any mistakes by hand. One font, one color pair from the palette (ink on cream, marigold or dusty rose).
6. Add one soft licensed track from CapCut/Instagram at low volume.
7. No fancy transitions. Plain cuts only.
8. Export 1080x1920 (vertical).

Rule: if you get stuck, post the simplest version. A rough one that's posted beats a perfect one that isn't.
