# Intro Reel v5: "The Niche Is Me" (English, own voiceover, B-roll, face here and there)

Built from her own words: people in the middle, the contradiction phase, crossroads and what-ifs, wanting everything and nothing, blocking everyone, more comfortable with strangers, Ved from Tamasha, small-town girl, "the niche is me."
No day counts. Business/jobs vague. Length about 50-55 sec (can be cut to 35).

---

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT

**1. Hook**
> There are some people who are stuck in the middle of things. In their contradiction phase.

**2. The contradiction**
> They want a quiet life. By the beach, or in the mountains.
> But they end up in the rush. Traffic everywhere. Deadlines. Always running.

**3. Her**
> I've always been at a crossroads. This way, or that way? This, or that?
> Some days I wonder: if I hadn't taken this road, would I have found these opportunities?
> And then: if I hadn't got distracted, would I have done better in the one thing I started?

**4. Everything and nothing**
> This is for the people who want everything, and also want nothing at all.

**5. Why it took so long**
> I've been trying to make this video for a very long time. I didn't know how this journey would start. So I waited. And procrastinated.
> I've also blocked almost everyone I know. I overthink, and I'm more comfortable with strangers. Here, I get to be anyone. Like Ved in Tamasha, in front of people who don't know me.

**6. Who she is**
> So, hi. A girl from a small town. Trying to make something of her life. Big, small, I don't know yet.

**7. The niche**
> People told me to pick a niche. But I've never walked one path. So the niche is me. And whatever else I find along the way.

**8. Close**
> If you're in the middle too, stay. There's room here.
