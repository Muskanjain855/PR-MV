## REEL 1: "The Middle" (25-30 sec, voiceover)

**Hook (0-3s)** on screen + voice:
> "Nobody tells you the hardest part isn't not knowing what you want. It's wanting something else while you're still doing this."

**Body**
> I run a small business from a small town. It's struggling. So I work a remote job to keep it alive.
> *(beat)*
> I want money. And I want to give it all away and live in the mountains.
> I want love. And I want to be free.
> I want my own house. And I want to be a nomad.
> *(beat)*
> I can't leave what I'm doing. And I can't stop wanting what I want.
> So I'm stuck in the middle. Maybe you are too.

**Turn**
> I used to wait for the perfect plan, the perfect shot, the perfect version of me. I'm the biggest procrastinator I know.
> So this is the imperfect version. Day 1.

**CTA**
> If this is you, send this to the one person who gets it. And tell me in the comments: what are you doing, and what do you want instead?

**On-screen text (big, typographic):** "doing this." / "wanting that." / "stuck in the middle?" / "Day 1"

### Shots (phone, natural light, no face needed)
1. Laptop + chai/coffee, hands typing (remote job)
2. Packing/labelling an order (business)
3. Gym: feet on treadmill / barbell close-up
4. Window shot, small-town street or rooftop
5. Dog in frame (one cute beat)
6. Optional single face shot at the end: "Day 1"
7. Overlay text over a mountain image/B-roll for the "mountains" line

### Edit notes (from references)
- Voiceover over B-roll, fast cuts on each contradiction (R10, R18)
- Big typography, one phrase on screen at a time (R03, R14)
- Freeze-frame + sticker/cartoon outline + retro effect on the final "Day 1" frame (R16 recipe, R05 style)
- Keep it a 1-hour edit: your own fonts and stickers (R05)
- Series label "Day 1" for accountability (R19, R02, R03, R13)
- Comment-prompt CTA (R05, R17, R20, R22)
- Caption: vulnerable, first-person, hashtags minimal (R07 tone)

### Caption (draft)
> I kept waiting for the perfect version of this. It never came, so here's the real one.
> If you're doing one thing and wanting another, you're not behind. You're in the middle. Stay here a while.
> Day 1. #introduction #figuringitout
