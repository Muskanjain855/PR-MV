# 100 B-Roll Ideas (reusable across reels)

Rules: phone vertical, near a window, 5-8 sec per clip, one wide + one close. No addresses, number plates, screens with private info, or other people's faces. Film once, reuse many times.

## Chai and home (1-12)
1. Chai being poured into a glass or cup
2. Steam rising from a cup near a window
3. Tea boiling in a pan, from above
4. Hands wrapped around a warm cup
5. Biscuit dunked in chai
6. A cup left half-finished and going cold
7. Morning light moving across the floor
8. Curtains moving in the breeze
9. Opening the window or balcony door
10. Fan spinning slowly, ceiling shot
11. Plants on the windowsill being watered
12. A lamp being switched on in the evening

## Dog (13-20)
13. Golden retriever napping, close-up on the paws
14. Dog looking out of the window
15. Your hand petting the dog
16. Dog walking ahead on a leash (from behind)
17. Dog's tail wagging at the door
18. Dog sitting next to you while you work
19. Dog yawning or stretching
20. Dog drinking water, close and slow

## Feelings and moments (94-100)

98. A hand turning a light off

100. Looking at your phone, then putting it down

## Quick shooting plan
- **One 90-minute session:** do all the chai, desk, wardrobe and dog clips (1-55).
- **One walk:** all the outdoor clips (64-85).
- **Craft session:** 86-93 whenever you pack or sketch.
- Store them in folders by category so you can reuse them.
