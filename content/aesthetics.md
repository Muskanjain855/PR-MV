# Page Aesthetics (from the "Thirty, Apparently" content bible)

**Direction:** warm documentary. Natural window light, candid pauses, imperfect rooms, ordinary gestures. No heavy filter. Consistency comes from type and layout, not one colour filter on everything.

## Palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Parchment | #EFE4D3 | backgrounds, title cards |
| Olive | #B5AD91 | secondary panels |
| Coffee | #715848 | accents, sub-text |
| Sage | #A7B2A1 | highlights, stickers |
| Charcoal | #3B302C | main text |

## Type and cards
- One reusable title-card style: charcoal text on parchment, same font every time (suggestion: an editorial serif for headlines, a clean sans for small text; not specified in the bible).
- Big text, one phrase at a time, left-aligned or centered consistently.

## Shooting
- Phone vertical 9:16. Face a window. Stack of books or cheap tripod.
- 5-8 sec per ordinary action, one wide and one close, then stop.
- Record voiceover separately like a voice note, lay it on the timeline first, then place B-roll over it.
- Simple cuts, auto-captions checked by hand, low-volume licensed music.
- Build a permanent library: tea, desk, books, dog, outfits, walking, textiles, window.

## Guardrails
- No live locations, addresses, licence plates, confidential screens, private finance or sensitive medical details.
- Don't force a lesson at the end. Don't manufacture sadness.
- Keep business sales and storytelling separate; disclose commercial links.
