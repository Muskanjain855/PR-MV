# Intro Reel v7: "Muskan, the Female Ved" (tight, hook-first, ~40 sec)

English. Narrator for the first part, her own voice for the last. No day counts. No repeated ideas.

## HOOKS
- **Visual hook (0-2s):** one hard cut on the beat. Left half: a mountain clip. Right half: her at a laptop late at night, tired. Big text: "WANTS: mountains / DOES: two jobs."
- **Script hook (0-4s):** "Meet her. Her name is Muskan. She thinks she's the female Ved from Tamasha."
- **Story hook (end of 4s):** "If this reel found you, you might be a little like her too." Then she walks off to find the storyteller (a question left open, answered at the end).

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT
> Hey, meet her. Her name is Muskan.
> She thinks she's the female Ved from Tamasha. And if this reel found you, maybe you're a little like her too.
>
> She wants to live in the mountains. She's working two jobs.
> She wants to feel loved. But the moment it's about giving someone a piece of her heart, it gets uncomfortable.
> She wants peace. She lives in a rush.
> She wants so many things, and does the exact opposite of all of them.
> And she's stuck in things she doesn't want to do, with no idea how to get out.
>
> So she went looking for the storyteller. "How does my story end?"
> And he smiled. "That's not mine to tell. It's yours to write."
>
> *(her own voice now)*
> So I thought: fine. I'll write it. Out loud. In front of you.
> The good days, the messy ones, the ones I get wrong.
> To see if the life I dream of, a quiet one, no money worry, work I love, is one I can actually have.
>
> I don't know how this ends. But if you're a little bit Ved too, stay. There's room here.

## WHY THIS SHARES
The WANTS / DOES format makes each line a screenshot-able card. People tag the friend who is "wants peace, does rush." The Ved reference gives a quick identity ("I'm a little Ved too"), and the storyteller question creates a payoff at the end.
