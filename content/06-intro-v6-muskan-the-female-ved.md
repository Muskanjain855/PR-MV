# Intro Reel v6: "Muskan, the Female Ved" (English, narrator then her own voice)

Structure: narrator introduces her in third person (Imtiaz Ali / Gulzar warmth), then she steps in with her own voice for the last lines.
No day counts. Business/jobs vague. About 55-60 sec (a 35 sec cut is marked).
Film reference: Tamasha (Ved, the storyteller). Lines are original; no dialogue copied.

---

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT

**1. Why this reel found you** (soft, direct to camera or over a dark screen)
> Hey. Hi there.
> Maybe this reel found you because you feel a little like Ved from Tamasha.

**2. Meet her** (narrator, warm)
> Meet her. Her name is Muskan.
> She's like Ved. The female version.

**3. The exaggeration** (quick cuts, a little funny, a little sad)
> She wants to live in the mountains. She's working two jobs.
> She wants all the love in the world. She wants to feel safe, held, secure. And the moment anyone gets close, she wants to run.
> She wants peace. She lives in the rush.
> She wants so many things. And does the exact opposite of all of them.

**4. Stuck**
> She's stuck in things she doesn't want to do. And she doesn't know how to get out.

**5. The storyteller** (slower, quieter, music softens)
> One day she got frustrated. And she went looking for the storyteller.
> She asked him: "How does my story end?"
> And the storyteller smiled and said: "It's not mine to tell. It's yours to write."

**6. The decision** (her own voice now, not the narrator)
> So I thought: fine. I'll write it. Out loud. In front of you.
> The good days, the messy ones, the ones where I get it wrong.
> To see if the life I dream of, a quiet one, no money worry, work I love, is something I can actually have.

**7. Close**
> I don't know how this ends. But if you're a little bit Ved too, stay. There's room here.

### 35-sec cut
Keep lines 1, 2, 3 (shortened), 5, 6 (first two lines), 7.
