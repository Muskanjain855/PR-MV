Thirty, Apparently · Vision board for 2027

A little bit of everything, on purpose.

Peace and money. Mountains and a good bed to come home to. A page that sounds like her, a body that is listened to, and a life that doesn't need to be resolved before it gets lived.

Career · the page

A page that sounds like me.

Picture thisA corner by the window. A phone on a stack of books. A wall calendar with posting days circled in marigold, not crossed out in guilt. Chai going cold, on purpose, because the take is good.

2027: 3 posts a week for the full year (skipping Sundays). One long film a month. First paid collaboration. 1,000 people who actually watch to the end.

Business

The business that pays me back.

Picture thisHands wrapping a parcel at a clean table. A ledger page where the last column is finally green. One thing taken off my plate by someone else.

2027: One clear offer. Profit tracked monthly. Business and personal money in separate accounts. Help for one task.

Money · finances

Money that lets me sleep.

Picture thisA drawn mountain you colour in as savings grow. An envelope marked "Mountain Fund". A notebook with ₹ ____ written in your own handwriting.

2027: Emergency fund first, then a monthly investment on autopilot. Health and term insurance sorted. Fill in your own numbers.

Peace

Tea, finished while it's still hot.

Picture thisA window, a cup with steam, a book face-down on the arm of the chair, the dog's chin on your foot. No phone in the shot.

2027: A screen-free first hour. Sundays off from posting. One slow weekend a month with nothing scheduled.

Health · body

Strong, rested, listened to.

Picture thisTwo halves of one shelf: gym shoes and a water bottle on one side, a folded blanket and a library book on the other. Both are part of the plan.

2027: Strength training 3–4 days a week on good days, rest days that count as plan. Yearly check-ups booked. Measure energy and sleep, not a weight on a scale.

Travel

Annapurna, a train window, one dream city.

Picture thisBoots at the start of a trail. A line of prayer flags against a blue morning. A passport stamp and a notebook, and nobody asking where you'll be next.

2027: Annapurna Base Camp trek planned and trained for. One long solo train journey. One week in Milan, France or London: pick one.

House · the mountain home

A window with pines in it.

Picture thisA wall of books. A wooden window frame with pines behind it. A stove, a dog bed next to it, one good chair, and a desk where you write. Also: a bed in town that is yours, so you can be a nomad without losing your pillow.

2027: A house fund with a name. Scout one mountain town for two weeks. Decide: buy, build or rent first.

Car

A car that reaches the mountains.

Picture thisA reliable car on a viewpoint road. Luggage in the back, a dog on the seat, chai in the cupholder.

2027: Decide new or used. Set the down-payment goal and the monthly limit before you fall for the showroom.

Jewellery · style

Pieces I'll wear for twenty years.

Picture thisA flat-lay on a handloom cloth: gold jhumkas, a thin chain, oxidised silver, a watch. Beside it, a skirt, a barrel pant and a farshi salwar. All of it hers.

2027: One meaningful piece at each milestone, not on impulse. A capsule wardrobe that matches the person, not the version you'll be later.

Writing

Something with my name on it.

Picture thisA stack of printed pages with a chai ring on the top sheet. A pen. A first small zine or ebook, held in hands.

2027: One essay a week. Twelve of the best become a zine or ebook.

Love · home

Room to breathe, together.

Picture thisTwo chairs, one lamp, two cups. Both reading, both in the same room, neither asking the other to be someone else.

2027: A weekly hour together with phones away. A weekly hour alone, held as seriously.

Community

A few real people, and strangers who get it.

Picture thisA table with three chairs and a pin board of messages from people who said "same". A coffee date you didn't cancel.

2027: Meet one friend a month. Reply to every early comment. Be easy to find for the people who feel in the middle too.

What I read in you

The short version, from everything shared so far.

  • You want rich-girl money and retired-grandmother peace, both at once. That tension is the page.
  • You're a careful perfectionist who procrastinates, and you've blocked people you know so you can be honest with strangers.
  • You've been an engineering student, theatre artist, entrepreneur, mountain-dweller and professional. Thirty, apparently.
  • Your dreams: mountains, writing, Annapurna, a home of your own, work you love, no money worry.
  • Your style sits between old-school Indian and Gen Z: skirts, barrel pants, styled dresses, farshi salwar.
