## 1. How to pitch yourself (60 seconds)
"I understand how the grid keeps supply and demand balanced, why storage is becoming essential as solar grows, and how policy such as VGF, ESO and state renewable policies shapes projects. I have studied Madhya Pradesh's BESS tenders and pumped storage policy, and I would like to turn that into advisory work for utilities, developers and regulators."

## 2. Core topics (from your study sheet)
Frequency (50 Hz, 49.90 to 50.05 Hz band), DSM, peak hour and the duck curve, energy shifting, FDRE, co-located solar plus storage, ancillary services (primary, secondary, tertiary), black start, VGF, ACC PLI, RPO/RCO, ESO, FTM vs BTM, MP tenders and policies.

## 3. Extra topics interviewers like
**Market structure**
- Power exchanges: IEX, PXIL, HPX. Segments: Day-Ahead (DAM), Real-Time (RTM), Term-Ahead, Green markets. Market coupling is being rolled out. Check the current status.
- Price discovery by merit order. Clearing price, price cap (₹10/kWh on exchanges, verify the current cap).
- Bilateral PPAs vs short-term power. Short-term and medium-term contracts.

**Regulation and law**
- Electricity Act 2003. Section 62 (regulator-determined tariff) vs Section 63 (tariff discovered by bidding).
- Roles: CERC (central), SERCs such as MPERC (state), CEA (planning and standards), NLDC/RLDC/SLDC (system operators), CTUIL (transmission planning).
- Tariff terms: ARR, true-up, MYT (multi-year tariff), cross-subsidy surcharge, wheeling charges, additional surcharge.
- Open access and Green Energy Open Access Rules 2022. GNA regulations (General Network Access) for transmission access.
- ISTS charge waiver for qualifying renewable and storage projects (check the current cut-off dates).

**Discom health**
- AT&C losses, billing and collection efficiency, subsidy dependence. RDSS (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme) and smart meters. Time-of-Day (ToD) tariffs and why they make BTM storage attractive.
- MP has three regional discoms (East at Jabalpur, Central at Bhopal, West at Indore) and a bulk buyer, MPPMCL. Verify current names and structure.

**Resource adequacy and storage economics**
- Resource adequacy (CEA framework): is there enough capacity at the evening peak, not just enough energy?
- Storage terms: MW vs MWh, duration (2h, 4h), round-trip efficiency (roughly 85 to 90% for lithium iron phosphate), depth of discharge, cycles, degradation, C-rate, augmentation, availability guarantee, LCOS (levelised cost of storage).
- Chemistries: LFP (dominant now), NMC, sodium-ion (emerging), flow batteries. Pumped hydro as long-duration storage.
- Revenue stacking: energy arbitrage + ancillary services + capacity payments. Business models: BOO tenders (as in MP), tolling, merchant.
- Risks: tariff risk, cell price swings, import dependence (China), land and connectivity, offtaker credit risk (discom payments), fire safety.

**Other hot topics**
- Green hydrogen, carbon market (CCTS), RECs, peak-demand records and heatwaves, coal plants' flexibilisation, nuclear and SMR announcements, transmission bottlenecks and curtailment.

## 4. Likely questions and short answers
1. **Why does India need storage?** Solar peaks at noon, demand peaks in the evening. Storage shifts energy and provides fast grid support, so we can add more renewables without more coal.
2. **FTM or BTM, which is more bankable?** FTM with a long government-backed contract (like MPPMCL's 12-year agreement plus VGF). BTM depends on the customer's tariff and savings, so it needs a payback calculation.
3. **How does a battery earn money?** Arbitrage, ancillary services, capacity or availability payments, avoided demand charges (BTM).
4. **What does VGF do?** It cuts upfront cost (up to ₹18 lakh/MWh in the current scheme), so the tariff the discom pays falls.
5. **What is DSM and who bears it?** Charges for deviating from schedule, borne by the deviating entity (discoms, generators). Storage reduces the exposure.
6. **What could go wrong with a BESS tender?** Delays in connectivity, supply chain and import dependence, offtaker payment risk, performance guarantees and degradation.
7. **MP specifics?** Two BESS tenders (750 MW/1,500 MWh and 625 MW/1,250 MWh), Pumped Hydro Policy (12,000 MW by 2034-35), RE Policy 2025 (wheeling waiver), Morena solar plus storage (about 440 MW plus 880 MWh), Gandhi Sagar pumped storage.
8. **Why consulting?** Mix of data, regulation and project work, plus exposure to many clients.
